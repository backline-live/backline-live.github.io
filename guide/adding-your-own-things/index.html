Adding your own things

  • A scene or an effect: a COMP in /project1/scenes (or /project1/effects) with an out1 TOP. Give it a custom page with the pars you want to play and set each par's slider range (normMin / normMax): that is the knob's range. Read them inside with parent().par.Name. It shows up as a tile, its pars in Parameters and as MIDI targets (av.par.scene.<name>.<Par>). Only the scenes on the two buses cook, so a big library costs nothing.
  • A panel: a COMP in /project1/modules (or tagged avmodule): out1 = its preview, custom pars = its controls; pars named Azimuth / Elevation / Distance orbit with the mouse in the preview.
  • Any network of yours, controllable (no code): 1. Drag templates/corepars_template.tox into your project (next to core, or up to 3 levels below) and rename it, e.g. my_look. 2. Its custom page Controls = what you play. Add pars there: Float / Int (with a sensible slider range), Toggle, Menu, colour, Pulse (a one-shot button) or Momentary (a button you hold). 3. Use them inside with expressions like parent().par.Speed. Keep the pars themselves plain values: a par with an expression on it cannot be played. 4. Pars on a page named Setup stay private. 5. They appear as user.my_look.Speed …: MIDI learn, search, snapshots. Two COMPs with the same name: only the first works; rename the other. Renaming later means re-learning its MIDI links.
