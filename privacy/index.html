Polar and some of its providers are based in or use servers in the United States. Transfers rely on the mechanisms Polar documents (EU standard contractual clauses in its DPA; EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified). GitHub: same. You can ask me for a copy of the safeguards.

5. What the Software sends over the network (complete list)

Checked in the code (core/, av/, midi_ctrl/, lights/, shared/, web pages) on 2026-11-25:

  1. Licence service (Polar), release build only.* On activation, at Core start and on deactivation, over HTTPS: licence key, machine code, [computer label], organisation id. Answer: activation id, status, seats used. Runs in the background; a failure never stops a show (offline grace: EULA §2). Polar sees your IP.
  2. Fixture library (LIGHTS part, only if it ships). When you add a fixture type that is not on your computer, the Software downloads its definition (maker/model.json) from the Open Fixture Library on raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub). Only when you ask. GitHub sees your IP and the fixture name.
  3. MIDI controller library. Only when you open Setup › Controllers › Import from library, the Software lists and downloads controller descriptions from the open-audiovisual repository on api.github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub), with the client name av-infrastructure-midi. GitHub sees your IP and the controller name.
  4. Nothing else leaves your computer by itself. No update check, no telemetry, no crash report, no analytics, no web fonts, scripts or images loaded from the internet by the pages (they are all local).

Things that stay on your own network, by your choice, and never go to me: the Software's pages are served on your computer (ports 9980 and following) and, only if you switch Remote access on (off by default), to phones and tablets on your network, protected by pairing; DMX / Art-Net / sACN to your lights; MIDI; Ableton Link with other computers of your network; the network traffic of TouchDesigner itself (Derivative) is separate and not mine.

On your computer the Software keeps, in ~/.av_infrastructure/: after you activate a key (licence or trial key), the key, its activation id, its end date (trial key) and the date of the last good check; nothing is sent before you activate. Delete that folder (after deactivating) to remove it.
