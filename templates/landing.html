Four parts. Drag in the ones your show needs.

Each part is one .tox. Core comes first; then visuals only, lights only, or both.

AV

/project1/av

Scenes and effects are COMPs in your project, picked up automatically and played on preview / program.

  • T-bar, AUTO, CUT, master, fade to black
  • Effects on their own bus, with their own transition
  • Scene parameters become controls on the page

Lights

/project1/lights

Looks are COMPs that drive fixture groups. Play them per group, in a looks × groups grid, with a GO for everything cued.

  • Fixtures from the Open Fixture Library (OFL / GDTF), patch, groups, 3D stage
  • Flash, solo, kill; strobe, blinder, black
  • DMX out over USB, Art-Net or sACN (off until you switch it on)

MIDI

/project1/midi

Learn any control of the page onto any knob, fader or pad. Values pick up where they are: nothing jumps.

  • Profiles for Launch Control XL MK2, APC mini MK1, LPD8 MK2, computer keyboard
  • 27 more models from the open-audiovisual library, or build your own
  • LED feedback, focus knobs for the selected scene or look

Core

/project1/core

One page, in TouchDesigner's own window and in any browser: PERFORM to play, SETUP to prepare.

  • Lock and panic, tap tempo, Ableton Link
  • Show folders and show files, crash recovery
  • Layouts you can edit, with Reset per screen

The controller plays. The screen confirms.

PERFORM shows everything on air at a glance, readable in a dark venue. You can play a whole set without touching the mouse.

Your show stays a TouchDesigner network.

No black box: a scene is a COMP with an output, a look is a COMP with an out1 CHOP. Open one mid-show and change it; the system only does the plumbing.

Bring scenes you already have: drop them in scenes, they appear on the page.

/project1

 ├─ av         preview / program for visuals
 ├─ lights     fixtures, patch, DMX out
 ├─ midi       controllers, Learn, LEDs
 ├─ scenes     your scenes · one COMP each
 ├─ effects    your effects
 ├─ looks      your light looks · out1 CHOP
 └─ lightfx    your light effects

Made for performers who build in TouchDesigner.

For you if

  • You make your own visuals in TouchDesigner and want to play them like an instrument.
  • You run the visuals and a few lights yourself, at the gig, from a MIDI controller.
  • You improvise: the next scene is decided in the room, not on a timeline.

Not for you (yet) if

  • You don't use TouchDesigner: content is made as TD COMPs.
  • Your show runs from timecode or a fixed cue list.
