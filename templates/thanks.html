Your order is done. Polar emails you the receipt. The files and your licence key are in your Polar customer portal.

Next, five steps

  1. In the order email press Access purchase: this opens your Polar customer portal.
  2. In the portal download ziptie-<pack>-<version>.zip and unzip it into a folder that stays (not Downloads). Once per computer; new releases appear in the same portal.
  3. New TouchDesigner project: drag in core.tox only. Core asks once where the ziptie folder is.
  4. On the licence screen paste the key shown in the portal (also in the email) and press Activate: Core adds the parts your pack includes.
  5. Play.
